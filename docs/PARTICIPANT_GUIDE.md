# VIGIL Recorder Participant Guide

Use this guide to complete one VIGIL recording session.

## Step 1: Open the Website

Open the participant website link.

Box 1: Click `Start`.

## Step 2: Enter Your Name

Type your name in the `Name` field.

Box 2: Click `Continue`.

If you used this device before, you may see your name under `Recent names`. You can click it to continue faster.

## Step 3: Consent

Read the consent page.

Box 3: Check each consent box.

Box 4: Click `Continue`.

## Step 4: Participant Details

Answer the two short questions.

Box 5: Choose whether you are a native English speaker.

Box 6: Choose the device you are using for recording.

Box 7: Click `Continue`.

## Step 5: Microphone Test

Use this page to make sure your microphone works.

Box 8: Click `Record`.

Say the test sentence on the screen.

Box 9: Click `Stop`.

Box 10: Play the recording back.

If it sounds okay, click `Continue`. If it does not sound okay, click `Redo` and record again.

## Step 6: Recording Workspace

You will see four prompt sections:

1. `Prompt 1 - VIGIL Only`
2. `Prompt 2 - Phrase/Sentence + VIGIL`
3. `Prompt 3 - VIGIL + Phrase/Sentence`
4. `Prompt 4 - Negative Examples`

You can record as many examples as you like in each section.

## Step 7: Record Prompt 1

For Prompt 1, say only:

`VIGIL`

Box 11: Click `Record`.

Wait until the page shows `Recording`, then speak.

Box 12: Click `Stop`.

Box 13: Play it back.

Box 14: Click `Accept Recording` if the audio is good.

If it is not good, click `Redo` and record again.

## Step 8: Record Prompt 2

For Prompt 2, choose or type a sentence that includes `VIGIL` at the end or after a phrase.

Example:

`Hi VIGIL.`

Box 15: Click one example chip, or type your own sentence.

Box 16: Click `Record`.

Wait until the page shows `Recording`, then speak the exact sentence.

Box 17: Click `Stop`.

Box 18: Play it back.

Box 19: Click `Accept Recording` if the audio is good.

## Step 9: Record Prompt 3

For Prompt 3, choose or type a sentence that starts with or includes `VIGIL`.

Example:

`VIGIL, go back.`

Box 20: Click one example chip, or type your own sentence.

Box 21: Click `Record`.

Wait until the page shows `Recording`, then speak the exact sentence.

Box 22: Click `Stop`.

Box 23: Play it back.

Box 24: Click `Accept Recording` if the audio is good.

## Step 10: Record Prompt 4

Prompt 4 is for negative examples.

Do not say the exact word `VIGIL` in this section.

Good examples:

- `visual`
- `digital`
- `visible`
- `This is a visual input.`

Box 25: Click one example chip, or type your own negative word or sentence.

Box 26: Click `Record`.

Wait until the page shows `Recording`, then speak the exact word or sentence.

Box 27: Click `Stop`.

Box 28: Play it back.

Box 29: Click `Accept Recording` if the audio is good.

## Step 11: Review Accepted Recordings

Each prompt section has an `Accepted Recordings` table.

Box 30: Use playback to listen again.

Box 31: Click `Delete` if a recording is wrong or bad.

The number next to each example shows how many times that exact phrase has been recorded.

## Step 12: Finish the Session

When you are done recording, go to the bottom of the page.

Box 32: Click `Finish Recording`.

## Step 13: Submit the Session

Review the session summary.

Box 33: Click `Upload All & Submit Session`.

Wait until the upload is complete.

After upload, the session will appear in your workspace.

## Notes

- Speak naturally.
- Do not imitate another accent.
- For Prompt 2 and Prompt 3, the transcript must include the exact word `VIGIL`.
- For Prompt 4, the transcript must not include the exact word `VIGIL`.
- If the first page is slow, wait a little. The backend may be waking up.
